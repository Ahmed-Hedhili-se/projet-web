About NeuroGrid

We make AI compute simple.

NeuroGrid gives AI teams of every size access to powerful GPUs, with honest hourly pricing and expert advice on the right hardware.

Our mission

Every model deserves the right hardware.

Training and serving AI models should not require a hardware budget the size of a research lab. Too many teams overpay for GPUs they do not need, or wait weeks for capacity.

We built NeuroGrid to fix that: a fleet of modern GPUs you can book by the hour, and a recommender that tells you exactly which one fits your model.
